Dress Code

College Uniform

Summer and winter uniform requirements, with weather-based relaxations and the standard sports kit.

Summer Uniform

Girls (Beginners to V)

Navy blue shirt with sash, baby collars and half sleeves, front left pocket bearing college monogram, white shalwar (Beginners to II wear white socks/stockings), black shoes with white plain socks. White scarf or hijab may be worn for religious reasons.

Girls (VI to XII)

Navy blue shirt with slits, baby collar, full sleeves with cuffs, shoulder flaps and front left pocket bearing college monogram. White sash for Classes VI–X and white plain lawn dupatta/sash for Classes XI–XII. Black shoes with white plain socks.

Boys

White half-sleeve shirt with collar and college monogram on the front left pocket. Navy blue trousers (up to Class II: navy blue tropical shorts). College necktie, black shoes with laces, white socks. Classes I–II may wear Velcro shoes.

Sports Kit

Girls: White shalwar, blue half-sleeve shirt with men's collar, white/black gym shoes with white socks.
Boys: Blue shorts/trousers, white half-sleeve shirt, white/black gym shoes with white socks.

Winter Uniform & Relaxations

Girls

As above, with a navy blue blazer (single breast, twin button, patch pockets, slits on flanks) or navy blue V-neck pullover, full-sleeve white blouse/shirt. Up to Class II: white, preferably woollen, tights/leggings.

Boys

As above, with a navy blue blazer, navy blue V-neck pullover and full-sleeve white shirt; up to Class II: navy blue trousers.

Weather Relaxations

Track suit / sports rig may replace the regular uniform for a period when deemed appropriate; neckties may be temporarily discontinued during hot, sultry days; a gradual switch between half and full sleeves (with or without pullover/blazer) is permitted per weather conditions.

Policy & Conduct

General Guidelines for Students & Parents

Quality education is achieved through strong collaboration between parents and college management. The following guidelines are promulgated for information and compliance.

Gate Closure & Attendance Policy
  • College gates close at 0750 hrs (Monday–Friday) for the morning shift.
  • For the afternoon shift, gates close at 1350 hrs (Monday–Thursday) and 1450 hrs on Friday.
  • After 0800 hrs (morning) / 1400 hrs (afternoon), no student will be allowed to enter.
  • Latecomers and absentees are fined Rs. 100/- per instance.
Uniform Regulations
  • Students must attend college in a neat, tidy uniform with properly trimmed nails.
  • Boys are to maintain an appropriate short haircut.
  • No entry to Chemistry or Biology labs without a proper lab coat.
  • Navy blue cardigans/sweaters are mandatory in winter.
  • Students failing to comply will not be allowed onto the premises.
Students' Leave
  • A minimum of 80% attendance is mandatory for all students.
  • Leave is granted only in extreme emergencies and shall not exceed one week.
  • Applications for leave of more than one day must be addressed to the Principal and submitted in advance through the Class Teacher/Vice Principal, stating name, class, section, shift and contact number.
  • Sick leave requires a valid medical certificate.
  • Short leave during working hours is strictly not allowed except in emergencies with written parental permission.
  • Long leave, Umrah leave, and leave for relatives'/siblings' marriages is generally discouraged during the academic session.
  • Parents are required to sign all acknowledgements, circulars, and notices issued by the college management or teachers.
Payment of College Fee / Dues

College dues must be cleared by the 20th of each month. Dues may be paid through designated banks, KuickPay (ID: 02770 + GR Number, available from the class teacher), or via the College Portal fee voucher.

Names of students who fail to pay dues for three consecutive months will be struck off the College rolls without notice. Re-admission is subject to availability of seats and payment of the full re-admission fee.

Discipline

The College Administration reserves the right to warn, fine, suspend, expel, or terminate any student found guilty of, including but not limited to:

  • Cheating, immoral conduct, misbehaviour, quarrelling, abusive language, or wall chalking
  • Willfully damaging or defacing college property
  • Rudeness or disrespect towards faculty or staff
  • Smoking, vapes/e-cigarettes, nicotine pouches, or playing truant
  • Violating college rules, regulations, or official instructions
  • Habitual irregularity, unpunctuality, or improper dress
  • Bringing or using mobile phones/devices on campus (subject to confiscation, non-returnable)
  • Taking part in violent, disruptive, or agitation activities harmful to the institution or its students
  • Consistently showing poor academic performance coupled with an indifferent or negative attitude towards studies

The Discipline Committee may investigate all serious breaches of conduct and recommend appropriate disciplinary action; these recommendations are reviewed by the College Council before a final decision is taken. A student who receives three disciplinary warnings will be permanently terminated from the College.

Entry Pass for Student Vehicles

Students with a valid driving licence must obtain a vehicle pass from PNS Karsaz through the Admin/Security Office before bringing any vehicle onto campus. Cars or motorcycles without a valid licence are not permitted, and violations are dealt with under Greater Karsaz Area (GKA) regulations.

Use of Social Media

Parents are advised to guide and monitor their children's social media activity, particularly late-night use that affects health and academic performance. Social media accounts are subject to monitoring by concerned agencies — content posted against Pakistan Navy or the College may result in strict disciplinary action, including expulsion.

Parents' Visit

Parents are to take a prior appointment on telephone to avoid any inconvenience, and may give suggestions for improvement or feedback about the college during these meetings. Parents are not allowed to visit classrooms directly to meet concerned teachers; if considered appropriate, the Vice Principal may arrange a meeting with the concerned teacher in his/her office.

Parents may also meet the Principal on working days, subject to availability. It is advisable to take a telephone appointment in advance. Principal's office hours (subject to availability):

  • Morning: Monday–Thursday 1000–1200 hrs, Friday 1000–1100 hrs
  • Afternoon: Monday–Thursday 1400–1530 hrs
Web Portal

Bahria College Karsaz's online web portal (www.bahriacolleges.edu.pk) gives parents anytime access to attendance records, fee vouchers, homework/assignments, and academic performance.

Send-up / Pre-Board Examinations

Students unsuccessful in Send-Up examinations will not be permitted to appear in Board Examinations. A student scoring less than 45% in any subject or less than 50% aggregate will not sit the Board Examination unless the deficiency is cleared in a subsequent exam.

Details of performance and eligibility will be communicated to parents through letters, circulars, or Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) prior to the commencement of Board Examinations. Parents are requested to collect the result and marked answer scripts of their child on the PTM or immediately after. Re-Send-Up examinations are not conducted except in extreme medical cases with an authentic medical certificate.

Summer Vacation

Summer Camp

Bahria College Karsaz is committed to the holistic development of its students by giving due importance to sports and physical activities at all educational levels. The College organizes an annual Summer Sports Camp during the summer vacation, providing an excellent opportunity to remain physically active, develop sporting skills, and engage in healthy recreational activities.

In addition, in collaboration with M/s Saylani Welfare International Trust and other renowned organizations, the College organizes short-term IT and skill development courses during the summer vacation, tailored to different age groups from Class I to Class XII — promoting digital literacy, innovation, and lifelong learning.
