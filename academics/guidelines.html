Policy & Conduct

Quality education is achieved through strong collaboration between parents and college management. The following guidelines are promulgated for information and compliance.

Students' Code of Conduct

At Bahria College Karsaz, discipline, integrity and respect are the cornerstones of academic excellence and character development. Every student is expected to uphold the highest standards of honesty, responsibility and personal conduct while contributing to a safe, respectful and conducive learning environment. As ambassadors of the College, students are expected to uphold its dignity and reputation both on and off campus.

  • Exemplary Conduct: Uphold the highest standards of integrity, honesty, self-discipline, responsibility and respect; observe all College rules in letter and spirit; comply with the instructions of teachers, staff and student appointment holders. Rudeness, bullying, fighting, gossiping, habitual tardiness or any conduct that disrupts the academic environment or brings disrepute to the College is strictly prohibited.
  • Punctuality & Attendance: Attend all classes, assemblies, examinations and College activities on time. Leaving the premises during working hours without prior permission is strictly prohibited.
  • Uniform & Personal Appearance: Wear the prescribed uniform with pride and maintain a neat, clean and well-groomed appearance. Boys must maintain an appropriate haircut.
  • Academic Integrity: Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, use of unfair means in examinations, submission of false information, forgery, or misuse of College documents/records are serious offences that may lead to expulsion.
  • Care for College Property: Deliberate damage, vandalism, theft, or misuse of College resources is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action, including recovery of damages.
  • Cleanliness: Help maintain a clean and green campus by avoiding littering, damaging plants, or walking across landscaped areas.
  • Participation & Responsibility: Participate actively in academic, co-curricular, sports and extracurricular activities, fostering leadership, teamwork, discipline and holistic development.
  • Prohibited Items: Mobile phones, unauthorized electronic gadgets, valuable jewellery, cameras, tobacco, vaping devices, drugs, alcohol, weapons, or other prohibited articles must not be brought onto campus — strict disciplinary action, including expulsion, may follow.
  • Upholding College Reputation: Students are ambassadors of the College and must uphold its dignity and reputation at all times, on and off campus.
Dress Code

The College uniform reflects the identity, discipline and values of Bahria College Karsaz. All students must attend College in the prescribed uniform and maintain a neat, clean and presentable appearance at all times; non-compliance may result in denial of entry to classes and disciplinary action including a fine. Montessori Beginners attend in civvies.

Full uniform specifications (summer, winter and sports kit by class/gender) are detailed on the Uniform page.

Student Identity Cards
  • All students are issued a College Identity Card, which must be carried at all times during College hours and produced upon request by any authorized member of the College administration.
  • Identity cards are subject to periodic verification during the academic session. Students must safeguard their card and immediately report any loss or damage to the College Administration.
  • A replacement card will be issued upon payment of the prescribed fee. A fine of Rs. 300 is charged for the loss or renewal of a College Identity Card.
Turn Out of Students

Students are expected to maintain a neat, smart and disciplined appearance at all times — attending College in the prescribed uniform with proper grooming, clean attire and neatly trimmed nails. Boys are required to maintain a proper short haircut. Students attending Chemistry and Biology practical classes must wear the prescribed laboratory coat, while navy blue cardigans or sweaters are mandatory during the winter season. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.

College Routine & Gate Timings

College follows a structured daily routine; the College reserves the right to revise the schedule when required.

Full daily class schedule and gate timings are detailed on the College Timings page.

Class Attendance

Every student is required to maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in each subject during the academic session. Students failing to meet this requirement may not be permitted to appear in the College Annual Examination and/or the relevant Board/Cambridge examinations. Parents are expected to ensure their child's regular attendance and discourage unnecessary absenteeism.

Students' Leave
  • Parents are requested to seek leave only under unavoidable circumstances/extreme emergencies to ensure students maintain the required 80% attendance.
  • Vice Principals are authorized to grant leave for up to three (3) days; leave exceeding three days requires the Principal's approval.
  • Leave for reasons other than sudden illness or emergencies must be applied for well in advance, stating the student's name, class, section, shift and contact number.
  • A student absent without approved leave for six (6) consecutive days or more is liable to have their name struck off the College rolls.
  • Sick leave is granted only upon submission of a valid medical certificate.
  • Short leave during College hours is not permitted except in genuine emergencies, with prior written parental consent and College approval.
  • Long leave, including for Umrah or family functions such as relatives'/siblings' marriages, is generally discouraged during the academic session.
College Fee / Dues

The fee structure is approved by the Board of Governors and reviewed periodically. Dues must be paid on or before the 10th of each month, through designated banks or KuickPay (ID: GR Number). Delay in payment may result in a late fee fine. Names of students failing to pay dues for three consecutive months will immediately be struck off the College rolls without notice; re-admission is subject to seat availability, payment of all outstanding dues and the re-admission fee.

  • Students submitting duly verified fee receipts from their previous school at admission may be charged tuition fee only for the remaining months of the academic year.
  • Students transferring between Bahria Colleges — Pakistan Navy wards and civilians alike — are exempt from the admission fee.
  • A Pakistan Navy ward taking fresh admission during an ongoing academic year pays the prescribed admission fee, but tuition fee for the preceding months is not charged.
  • Upon transfer between Bahria Colleges, the security deposit is refunded by the previous institution and deposited at the new one.
Fee Concessions

Fee concessions are admissible subject to verification of eligibility and submission of the prescribed supporting documents:

  • Children of personnel supporting Pakistan Navy (FA (Navy), CNA, MES, strategic organizations funded through Defence Estimates and permanent Bahria Colleges employees) — concession ceases upon transfer to another ministry/organization; continues at the concessional rate for the remainder of the academic year in the event of the parent's retirement or death.
  • Children of Bahria College employees — full tuition fee concession for the first child; 25% concession for the second child (allied charges payable in full). For Cambridge O/A-Level, tuition fee is charged at the Naval rate and allied charges at the Civilian rate.
  • Second child of serving Pakistan Navy personnel — 30% tuition fee concession in the Matric stream (not applicable to Cambridge O/A-Level).
  • Legally adopted child of Pakistan Navy personnel — extendable to one legally adopted child of a married service member with no biological child, subject to approval through Naval Headquarters (Directorate of Naval Education).
Selection of Student Appointment Holders

The Principal may constitute a Selection Committee — comprising the respective Vice Principals, House Masters/Mistresses and at least two senior faculty members — to recommend students for College Captain/Head Girl, Deputy College Captain/Deputy Head Girl, House Captains and Prefects. Selection is based on:

  • A consistently good academic record (need not be the highest achiever, but among high performers)
  • Demonstrated leadership potential, integrity, moral character and an impressive personality
  • Exemplary discipline, conduct and personal turnout
  • Confidence, sound judgment, independent thinking and decision-making ability
  • Good reputation, responsible behaviour and respectful interpersonal relations
  • Length of association with the College and understanding of its traditions and values
  • Effective communication and interpersonal skills
  • Maturity, fairness and the ability to exercise authority responsibly
House System

To foster healthy competition, teamwork, leadership and a strong sense of belonging, BCKz follows a House System — every student is assigned to one of six Houses:

Jinnah House — Green Iqbal House — Red Tipu Sultan House — Maroon Zafar House — Yellow Sir Syed House — Navy Blue Liaquat House — Purple

Each House comprises students from different classes for balanced participation across age groups, competing throughout the year in academic, sports, literary, cultural and other co-curricular activities. Students wear their House Badge as part of the prescribed uniform and are encouraged to uphold their House's traditions and spirit.

Use of Social Media

Parents are advised to monitor and guide their children's use of social media and other digital platforms; unnecessary late-night use should be discouraged as it may affect health, well-being and academic performance.

Students are expected to use social media responsibly and must not post, share, or circulate content that is false, offensive, defamatory, discriminatory, or prejudicial to the dignity, security, or reputation of the Pakistan Navy, Bahria College Karsaz, its students, faculty or staff. Misuse or online conduct that violates College rules may result in disciplinary action, including suspension or expulsion.

Acts of Indiscipline

Any act that violates College Rules or disrupts the academic environment is treated as an act of indiscipline, including but not limited to:

  • Disobedience or defiance of College rules, regulations, or lawful instructions
  • Misconduct, including rudeness, bullying, harassment, fighting, gossiping, intimidation, or disrespect towards staff, fellow students, or visitors
  • Use of unfair means in examinations, plagiarism, forgery, or misuse of College documents/records
  • Cheating, immoral or gross misconduct, quarrelling, abusive language, or wall chalking
  • Intentional disruption of teaching, examinations, assemblies, or other College activities
  • Willful damage, theft, or misuse of College property and facilities
  • Possession or use of prohibited items — tobacco, vaping devices, drugs, alcohol, weapons, or unauthorized electronic devices
  • Habitual absenteeism, late arrival, unauthorized absence, or leaving the premises without permission
  • Failure to wear the prescribed uniform or maintain proper grooming, including haircut
  • Persistent neglect of academic responsibilities or consistently poor performance coupled with an indifferent attitude
  • Smoking, playing truant, or use of mobile phones on College premises
  • Participating in violent or agitational activities harmful to the institution or its students
  • Habitual irregularity, unpunctuality, untidiness, or improper dress
  • Any conduct, on or off campus, that undermines discipline or compromises the safety or reputation of the College
Disciplinary Measures

Violations of College Rules, whether on campus or during educational visits, competitions, tours, or other College activities, are dealt with firmly and fairly. Depending on the nature and severity of the offence, one or more of the following measures may be imposed: fine; verbal or written warning; withdrawal of privileges, responsibilities, or participation in College activities; recovery of the cost of damages or losses; suspension for a specified period; or expulsion from the College. The College Management reserves the right to impose any disciplinary measure deemed appropriate.

Parents' Visits & Parent-Teacher Meetings

Parents are encouraged to maintain regular communication with the College regarding their child's academic progress, conduct and development and may meet the respective Vice Principal to discuss matters or offer feedback. Routine or minor matters may be discussed by telephone. Direct visits to classrooms or meetings with teachers are not permitted — if considered appropriate, the Vice Principal may arrange a meeting with the concerned teacher.

Parents may meet the Principal, subject to availability or prior appointment, during the following office hours:

  • Monday–Thursday: 1000–1200 hrs (morning), 1400–1530 hrs (afternoon)
  • Friday: 1000–1100 hrs (morning), 1400–1500 hrs (afternoon)

Parent–Teacher Meetings give parents the opportunity to discuss their child's academic progress, attendance, conduct and development with teachers and the Vice Principal. Parents are expected to attend punctually, participate with courtesy and mutual respect, maintain confidentiality and work collaboratively with the College by considering teachers' recommendations.

Parents are expected to interact with College staff respectfully and courteously at all times. Inappropriate behaviour towards Vice Principals, faculty or staff will be formally recorded in the student's personal record and serious misconduct may be referred to the Administrative Authority — potentially resulting in withdrawal of the student from the College.

General Instructions
  • Unauthorized visitors are not permitted to meet students on the College premises, except with the Principal's or respective Vice Principal's authorization.
  • Students shall not bring visitors or outsiders onto campus without the Principal's prior approval.
  • Smoking, vaping and the use of any tobacco products are strictly prohibited on the College premises for students, parents, guardians, visitors and all other persons.
  • Students are not permitted to leave the premises during College hours except in exceptional circumstances with prior approval.
  • The College encourages participation in academic, literary, cultural, scientific, religious, sports and co-curricular activities; all forms of students'/teachers' union activities are strictly prohibited.
  • Distribution, display, or circulation of unauthorized pamphlets, notices, posters, or printed material on the premises is prohibited.
  • Students are expected to use social media responsibly and ethically.
Awards & Recognition

Board Position Holders — students securing top positions in the SSC and HSSC Board Examinations are awarded:

  • First Position — Chief of the Naval Staff (CNS) Gold Medal and a cash prize of Rs. 35,000
  • Second Position — Chairman BoG Bahria Colleges Gold Medal and a cash prize of Rs. 25,000
  • Third Position — President, Managing Committee, Bahria Colleges Gold Medal and a cash prize of Rs. 15,000

Annual Trophies & Certificates:

  • Chief of the Naval Staff Trophy — Best House of the year, overall performance
  • Chairman BoG Academic Trophy — House with the highest aggregate academic points
  • Commander Karachi Sports Trophy — House with the highest aggregate sports points
  • Best Student Trophy — outstanding overall achievement in academics, discipline, conduct, leadership, sports and co-curricular activities
  • General trophies and cups — winning teams and individuals in sports, literary, cultural and other competitions
  • Certificates of merit and prizes — distinguished academic performance and outstanding achievement
