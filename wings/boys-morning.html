About This Wing

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Established in March 2002, the Boys Wing (Morning Shift) is a key academic component of Bahria College Karsaz, committed to academic excellence, character building and leadership development. Affiliated with the Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Islamabad and the Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi, the Wing provides Secondary and Higher Secondary education in a disciplined and academically enriching environment. It comprises 23 classrooms and accommodates more than 700 students.

The Wing offers a diverse range of academic programmes to support students' educational and career aspirations. At the Secondary level, students may opt for Biology or Computer Science. At the Higher Secondary level, programmes are offered in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering and General Science under both the Federal and Karachi Boards, whereas Commerce is offered under the Karachi Board.

Renowned for its strong academic tradition, the Boys Wing has consistently achieved outstanding Board results. The dedication of its students and the commitment of a highly qualified 42-member teaching staff have enabled the Wing to maintain a distinguished record of academic achievement and excellence.

Wing Leadership

Message from the Vice Principal

Mr. Imran Safdar Vice Principal, Boys Wing (Morning)

Welcome to the Boys Wing of Bahria College Karsaz. At the Boys Wing, we are committed to nurturing responsible, confident and disciplined young men through quality education and holistic development. Alongside academic excellence, we emphasize character building, leadership, integrity and a strong sense of responsibility.

Our students consistently achieve outstanding Board results, reflecting the dedication of both faculty and learners. Equal importance is given to co-curricular, extracurricular and sports activities, which help develop leadership, teamwork, confidence and resilience. The success of the Boys Wing is a testament to the collective efforts of our students, teachers and parents, who together uphold the proud traditions of Bahria College Karsaz.

Academic Streams

Programmes Offered

Five distinct HSSC programmes under Federal Board and Karachi Board, designed to prepare students for top universities.

Pre-Medical

Biology, Chemistry, Physics & English — pathway to MBBS, BDS and health sciences.

Pre-Engineering

Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry & English — foundation for engineering, architecture and technology degrees.

General Science

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics & English under both Federal and Karachi Boards — a broad-based science foundation.

Commerce

Accounting, Business Studies, Economics & English under the Karachi Board — preparation for BBA, CA, ACCA and finance careers.

Examination Authority

Affiliated Board

Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Islamabad & Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi

The Boys Wing (Morning) is affiliated with the Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Islamabad and the Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi for Secondary and Higher Secondary examinations. These boards are responsible for conducting examinations, issuing certificates and maintaining academic standards. Their comprehensive syllabi and rigorous assessment framework ensure that BCKz students are well-prepared for higher education.
