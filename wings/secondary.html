About This Wing

Secondary Wing (Afternoon)

The Secondary Wing (Afternoon) was established in 2018 to accommodate the growing demand for admissions at Bahria College Karsaz. Initially affiliated with the Karachi Board, the Wing expanded its academic offerings in 2020 with the introduction of SSC classes under the Federal Board.

The Wing caters to both boys and girls of Classes VI to X. Since its establishment, it has remained committed to academic excellence by providing a disciplined, supportive, and student-centred learning environment that promotes strong educational foundations, character development, and personal growth.

At the Secondary level, students may opt for Biology or Computer disciplines under both the Federal and Karachi Boards, preparing them for a smooth transition into the Higher Secondary programmes offered across the College's morning and afternoon wings.

Message from the Vice Principal

Ms. Sadia Khanam Vice Principal, Secondary Wing

The secondary years are a crucial stage in shaping students' academic and personal development. At Bahria College Karsaz, we are committed to providing a disciplined and supportive learning environment that promotes academic excellence, character building, leadership, and personal growth.

Guided by our core values of Faith, Character, Courage, and Commitment, we strive to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and values required to succeed in higher education and become responsible citizens. It is a privilege to be part of an institution renowned for its tradition of excellence and character development.

Academic Disciplines

Disciplines Offered

Two disciplines under both the Federal and Karachi Boards, available to both boys and girls of Classes VI–X.

Biology

Offered under both the Federal and Karachi Boards — the foundation stream for students pursuing Pre-Medical at Higher Secondary level.

Computer

Offered under both the Federal and Karachi Boards — building the technical foundation for Computer Science and Pre-Engineering streams.

Humanities (Girls)

With Economics and Food & Nutrition, offered exclusively under the Federal Board for girls of the Secondary Wing.
