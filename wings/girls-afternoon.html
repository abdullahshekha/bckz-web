About This Wing

Established in 2020, the Girls Wing (Afternoon Shift) is an integral part of Bahria College Karsaz, dedicated to academic excellence and character development. Affiliated with the Federal and Karachi Boards, the Wing offers Intermediate-level programmes in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, General Science, Commerce and Humanities. The Humanities stream is offered exclusively under the Karachi Board and includes Psychology, Sociology and English Elective as optional subjects.

The Wing emphasizes conceptual understanding, critical thinking and analytical skills. Its consistently outstanding Board results reflect the dedication and hard work of both students and faculty. Modern classrooms, well-equipped science laboratories and a resource-rich library provide an environment conducive to effective learning and academic achievement.

Supported by a qualified and committed faculty of 40+ teaching staff, the Wing fosters a student-centred learning culture that promotes confidence, discipline, leadership and social responsibility, preparing students for higher education and productive roles in society.

Wing Leadership

Message from the Vice Principal

Dear Students, Parents and Members of the Bahria Family, it gives me great pleasure to welcome you to the Girls Wing (Afternoon Shift) of Bahria College Karsaz. At our Wing, education extends beyond academic achievement. We are committed to nurturing confident, capable and principled young women through a balanced focus on academic excellence, character development and personal growth.

Through quality education, dedicated mentorship and a disciplined learning environment, we strive to equip our students with the knowledge, skills and values required for success in higher education and life. Alongside academics, we place great emphasis on leadership, ethical values and social responsibility to ensure the holistic development of every student.

Academic Streams

Programmes Offered

Five distinct SSC and HSSC programmes under Federal Board and Karachi Board, designed to prepare students for top universities.

Pre-Medical

Biology, Chemistry, Physics & English — pathway to MBBS, BDS and health sciences.

Pre-Engineering

Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry & English — foundation for engineering, architecture and technology degrees.

General Science

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics & English — a broad-based science foundation under both Federal and Karachi Boards.

Commerce

Accounting, Business Studies, Economics & English — preparation for BBA, CA, ACCA and finance careers.

Humanities (Karachi Board)

Psychology, Sociology & English Elective — offered exclusively under the Karachi Board.

Examination Authority

Affiliated Boards

Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi & Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Islamabad

The Girls Wing (Afternoon) is affiliated with both the Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi for HSC-level examinations and the Federal Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Islamabad for SSC-level classes. These affiliations ensure that students benefit from nationally recognised qualifications, rigorous syllabi and assessment frameworks that open doors to top universities across Pakistan and abroad. BCKz prepares every student comprehensively for the requirements of both boards.
