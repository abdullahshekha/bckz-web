About This Wing

Boys Wing (Afternoon)

Boys Wing (Afternoon Shift), established in 2020, is a vibrant academic component of Bahria College Karsaz, committed to excellence in education and character building. Affiliated with the Federal and Karachi Boards, the Wing offers Intermediate-level programmes in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering and General Science. It comprises more than 20 classrooms and accommodates around 700 students.

The academic programme is designed to promote conceptual understanding, critical thinking and analytical skills. Consistently commendable Board results reflect the dedication of the students and the commitment of a highly qualified 40-member teaching staff. Modern classrooms, well-equipped science laboratories and a well-stocked library further support an effective and stimulating learning environment.

Through a student-centred approach, the Wing nurtures discipline, confidence, leadership and social responsibility. Alongside academic achievement, emphasis is placed on the holistic development of students, preparing them for higher education and meaningful contributions to society.

Message from the Vice Principal

Mr. Imran Safdar Vice Principal, Boys Wing (Afternoon)

Dear Students, Parents and Members of the Bahria Family, it gives me great pleasure to welcome you to the Boys Wing (Afternoon Shift) of Bahria College Karsaz. We are committed to providing quality education in a disciplined and supportive environment that fosters academic excellence, character building and personal growth. Our aim is to develop confident, responsible and principled young men equipped with the knowledge, skills and values required for success in higher education and life.

The consistent academic achievements of our students reflect the dedication of our faculty, the hard work of our learners and the unwavering support of parents. Alongside academics, we place strong emphasis on leadership, discipline, teamwork and social responsibility through a variety of co-curricular and extracurricular activities. Together, we remain committed to upholding the proud traditions of Bahria College Karsaz.

Academic Streams

Programmes Offered

Three distinct HSSC programmes under Federal Board and Karachi Board, designed to prepare students for top universities.

Pre-Medical

Biology, Chemistry, Physics & English — pathway to MBBS, BDS and health sciences.

Pre-Engineering

Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry & English — foundation for engineering, architecture and technology degrees.

General Science

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics & English under both Federal and Karachi Boards — a broad-based science foundation.

Examination Authority

Affiliated Board

Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi (BIEK)

The Boys Wing (Afternoon) is affiliated with the Board of Intermediate Education, Karachi (BIEK) for HSC Part-I and Part-II examinations. BIEK is responsible for conducting examinations, issuing certificates and maintaining academic standards for Intermediate-level institutions throughout Karachi. The board's comprehensive syllabi and rigorous assessment framework ensure that BCKz students are well-prepared for higher education.
