About This Wing

Established in 1997 alongside the inception of Bahria College Karsaz, the Primary Wing is dedicated to providing quality education to students from Classes I to V in an environment that promotes academic excellence, character building and holistic development. Guided by national aspirations and contemporary educational practices, the Wing equips students with the knowledge, skills and values required to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

From its humble beginning with just 22 students, the Primary Wing has grown into a vibrant centre of learning, renowned for nurturing the intellectual, social, emotional and physical development of every child. Through a balanced curriculum, dedicated faculty and a supportive learning environment, the Wing lays a strong foundation for lifelong learning, responsible citizenship and future academic success.

The foundation stone of the present Primary Wing building was laid on 17 July 2009 by Vice Admiral Saleem Ahmed Meerani, HI(M), Commander Karachi. The purpose-built facility was inaugurated on 6 April 2011 by Begum Zubaria Noman, President of the Pakistan Navy Women Association, following which the Wing was relocated to its present campus.

Message from the Vice Principal

Ms. Aqsa Noreen Vice Principal, Primary Wing

Welcome to the Primary Wing of Bahria College Karsaz. At the Primary Wing, we are committed to providing a safe, nurturing and stimulating environment where every child is valued, encouraged and inspired to achieve his or her full potential. We believe that the primary years are fundamental in shaping a child's academic, social and emotional development and we strive to instill curiosity, creativity, confidence and a lifelong love for learning.

Through a balanced curriculum, dedicated teachers and modern teaching practices, we promote academic excellence alongside character building, discipline and moral values. We firmly believe that the partnership between parents and educators is essential to a child's development and together we endeavour to nurture compassionate, resilient and responsible individuals prepared to contribute positively to society.
