About This Wing

Junior Wing

Established in 1997 alongside the inception of Bahria College Karsaz, the Junior Wing began its journey in three officers' bungalows with 190 students and nine teachers. Over the years, it has evolved into a vibrant centre for early childhood education. In February 2023, the Wing relocated to its present purpose-built campus, offering a modern and child-friendly learning environment.

The Junior Wing caters to Beginner, Advance and Prep classes, providing a strong foundation for lifelong learning in a safe, nurturing and stimulating setting. The curriculum emphasizes literacy, numeracy, communication, creativity and social development through age-appropriate, activity-based and experiential learning.

Supported by a team of dedicated and caring educators, students are encouraged to explore, discover and learn through hands-on experiences that foster curiosity, confidence and independent thinking. Alongside academic development, equal emphasis is placed on character building, discipline, moral values and social responsibility, ensuring the holistic development of every child during these formative years.

Wing Leadership

Message from the Vice Principal

Ms. Farah Sarfraz Vice Principal, Junior Wing

Welcome to the Junior Wing of Bahria College Karsaz, where every child's educational journey begins in a safe, nurturing and stimulating environment. We believe that early childhood is the foundation of lifelong learning. Our aim is to cultivate curiosity, creativity, confidence and independence through a child-centred approach that encourages exploration, discovery and experiential learning.

Our dedicated teachers provide individual attention and compassionate guidance, recognizing the unique abilities and learning pace of every child. Alongside academic readiness, we place strong emphasis on character building, moral values, empathy, discipline and respect, ensuring the holistic development of each learner. Together with parents as valued partners, we strive to nurture compassionate, confident and responsible young learners prepared for future academic success.

Academic Levels

Our Programmes

Three carefully structured tiers that guide young learners from their very first steps in education through to full school readiness.

Beginner

An introduction to structured learning through play, sensory exploration, and creative activities that build foundational skills.

Advance

Building on foundational skills with more structured activities, pre-reading, pre-writing, and early numeracy concepts.

Prep

Bridging the gap to Primary Wing with focused preparation in literacy, numeracy, and social skills for school readiness.
