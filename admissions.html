Admissions

Bahria College Karsaz invites applications for admission to Montessori Beginners during December/January (subject to approval by the competent authority), Class XI during April/May for both Morning and Afternoon Shifts, and Classes VI to X during February/March for the Afternoon Shift. Admission schedules are announced through the College's official website and social media platforms.

Classes in the Junior Wing, Primary Wing and Cambridge Wing are conducted exclusively in the Morning Shift, while Classes VI to XII are offered in both Morning and Afternoon Shifts.

Admissions to Prep to Class X (Morning Shift) and the Cambridge Stream (Pre-O Level, O Level and A Level) are offered subject to the availability of seats on a first-come, first-served basis. Admission to all classes is subject to the availability of seats, successful qualification of the prescribed Entrance Test and fulfillment of the College's eligibility criteria.

The shift allotted at the time of admission shall be final. Students admitted to the Afternoon Shift shall not be eligible for transfer to the Morning Shift under any circumstances.

Requirements, Procedure & Documents

Admission Details by Wing

Admission announcement timing, eligibility criteria, programmes, entrance test subjects, how to apply, required documents and important notes for each wing. All admissions are subject to seat availability and a qualifying entrance test.

Junior Wing (Montessori Beginners, Advance & Prep) — Morning Shift Only
Admission Announcement

Admissions for Beginners are announced on the College's official social media platforms during December–January every year. For admission in Advance & Prep classes, no formal announcement is made and admissions are subject to availability of seats only.

Eligibility Criteria

Age requirement (2.25–3.6 years for Beginners); qualifying the entrance test.

Entrance Test
  • Montessori Beginners: Speech & Motor Development Tests
  • Montessori Advance & Prep: English, Urdu, Numbers
How to Apply
  1. Obtain the admission prospectus and application form from the College's admissions office.
  2. Complete the application form and attach all required documents.
  3. Appear in the entrance test/assessment (see above).
  4. Admissions are processed on a rolling basis, subject to seat availability — no formal merit list is issued for Advance & Prep classes.
Documents Required
Birth Certificate / copy of Registration Form 'B' (for Montessori Beginners classes)
Two passport-size coloured photographs
School Leaving Certificate issued by previous institution
Result card (if applicable) issued by previous institution
Copy of National Identity Card of Parent/Guardian
CO's certificate for serving/retired uniform personnel of armed forces and PN Civilians and PAF 373 (Pension Book) for retired uniform personnel and PN Civilians

Important: Admissions to Montessori Beginners are restricted to children aged 2.25 to 3.6 years. Overage students are not entertained. Montessori admissions are generally conducted in December/January, subject to approval from higher authorities and availability of seats.

Higher Secondary Wing — HSSC, Federal & Karachi Boards (Classes XI–XII) — Both Shifts
Admission Announcement

Admissions are announced on the College's official social media platforms during April–May every year.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum 65% marks in SSC-I and 70% in O-Level or equivalent are required. Merit is determined on the basis of 60% Class IX marks and 40% entrance test score. Admission is provisional, subject to securing at least 65% marks in SSC or 70% in O-Level or equivalent examination.

Entrance Test
  • Pre-Engineering: English, Physics, Chemistry, Math
  • Pre-Medical: English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Computer Science: English, Physics, Chemistry, Computer/Biology
  • Humanities: English, General Math, General Science
  • Commerce: English, Math

Note: For Boys, the Commerce group is offered under the Karachi Board in the morning shift only. Humanities group is offered in both shifts for girls only.

Cambridge Wing (Pre-O Level, O-Level & A-Level) — Morning Shift Only
Admission Announcement

No formal announcement is made for admissions in Pre-O Level & A-Level; admissions are subject to availability of seats. For A-Level, admissions are announced on the College's official social media platforms during May–June every year.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum 60% marks in the previous class; minimum 60% marks in each subject of the Entrance Test. For A-Level, students with a minimum of 1×A, 1×B, 1×C will be eligible to apply.

Entrance Test
  • Classes VI–VIII (P-I, II & III): Urdu, English, Math, Science
  • Class IX (SR-I): Urdu, English, Math, Science
  • Class X (SR-II): All subjects of the previous class
  • A-Level: Interview-based (min. 1×A, 1×B, 1×C at O-Level)

College offers Ordinary and Advanced Level courses of Cambridge University, being registered with the British Council. Students from Class VI onward are bifurcated into Matric and O-Level streams depending upon their options, available vacancies and qualifying admission test. Only students with good academic potential to pursue the intensive curriculum are given the chance to apply for O & A-Level.

How to Apply
  1. Obtain the admission prospectus and application form from the College's admissions office.
  2. Complete the application form, selecting the O-Level or A-Level stream, and attach all required documents.
  3. Appear in the entrance test (see above) — a minimum of 70% marks per subject is required to qualify for O-Level; A-Level admission is interview-based.
  4. Check the merit list at the college notice board / official social media accounts (O-Level).
  5. Complete admission formalities and deposit the prescribed fee within the stipulated time.
Documents Required
Birth Certificate / copy of Registration Form 'B' (for Montessori Beginners classes)
Two passport-size coloured photographs
School Leaving Certificate issued by previous institution
Result card (if applicable) issued by previous institution
Copy of National Identity Card of Parent/Guardian
CO's certificate for serving/retired uniform personnel of armed forces and PN Civilians and PAF 373 (Pension Book) for retired uniform personnel and PN Civilians

Important: Students in the O-Level stream who show academic deficiencies and clear signs of difficulty in coping with the curriculum may be transferred to the Matric stream, subject to seat availability. Such transfers are only possible up to Class VIII of the O-Level (P-III level). No transfers are entertained from O-Level to Matric stream at Class IX level or above.
